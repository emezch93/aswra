Why ASWRA membership exists

Membership connects eligible individuals and organisations working across the waste recovery and recycling value chain to a shared platform for organisation, capacity development, industry cooperation and advocacy. ASWRA aims to strengthen its members through relevant information, training opportunities, constructive stakeholder engagement and facilitated market or partnership linkages where available.

Membership is subject to the Association’s constitution, approved policies, category requirements and review process. The opportunities described on this page are not guaranteed benefits or entitlements.

Membership categories

Individual Recycler Members

Waste collectors and recovery workers, informal waste sector operators, independent recyclers and aggregators, and individuals engaged in collecting and recovering recyclable materials.

Recycling Business Members

Micro, small and medium recycling enterprises, plastic, metal, paper, glass and other material recovery businesses, recycling processors and aggregators, and waste collection and resource recovery businesses.

Cooperative Members

Registered cooperatives operating in the waste and recycling sector, including eligible thrift and multipurpose cooperative societies.

Institutional Members

Eligible non-governmental organisations, community based organisations, environmental organisations and other institutions whose activities align with ASWRA's objectives.

Strategic and Corporate Partners

Recycling companies and material off takers, producers and organisations in extended producer responsibility initiatives, private sector organisations supporting responsible waste management, and development partners.

Requirements

To qualify as an active individual recycler member, an applicant must, according to the Association's membership rules:

  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Live in Anambra State or have an eligible operational connection with the State.
  • Be engaged in, or have a legitimate interest in, waste collection, recovery, aggregation, recycling or related activities.
  • Complete the approved registration and onboarding process, including the Recyclers Academy induction training or an approved equivalent.
  • Provide a working telephone number, the identification documents the Association requires and a guarantor where required.
  • Agree to follow the ASWRA Constitution, approved policies, Code of Conduct and lawful decisions.
  • Pay approved registration fees and other properly authorised charges, where applicable.

Corporate, institutional and cooperative applicants must provide the registration and organisational documents required for their category.

About this online form.

The online application collects only the basic information needed to start your application. If further information, documents, onboarding steps or approved fees are required, the ASWRA administrator will contact you through an official channel.

Do not send your NIN, identity documents or any payment with this form. Pay only through officially approved channels and keep your receipt.

Membership benefits

Registered members may access these opportunities, subject to the Association's rules, programme availability and eligibility.

Information

Recycling markets, industry developments, technologies and opportunities.

Training

Approved workshops, seminars and professional development.

Networking

Connect with recyclers, enterprises, producers, off takers and institutions.

Market access

Facilitated links with legitimate buyers, aggregators and processors.

Cooperative opportunities

Savings, credit or grants through duly established arrangements, subject to their requirements.

Advocacy

Collective concerns raised with relevant authorities.

Digital services

Approved ASWRA HOME services for membership administration and communication.

Business development

Information, advisory support and referrals.

Programmes

Consideration for relevant government, private sector or development partner programmes.

Recognition

Approved industry events, exhibitions and awareness activities.

Important. Membership does not guarantee loans, grants, contracts, government appointments, producer payments or admission into any third party programme. ASWRA membership does not give anyone police, regulatory, tax collection or enforcement powers, and does not exempt anyone from lawful taxation, regulation or environmental requirements.

How to apply

  1. Choose your category and complete the online form below.
  2. Receive your reference number as soon as you submit. Keep it safe.
  3. ASWRA reviews your application. You can follow progress on the application status page.
  4. ASWRA reviews your application. If more information or a decision needs to be communicated, the authorised administrator will contact you manually through the Association’s approved contact channel.
  5. Check your application status with your reference number. A status change does not mean an email was automatically sent.

What the statuses mean

Pending
Received and awaiting review.
Under Review
Being examined by ASWRA.
Requires Information
ASWRA needs more information from you.
Recommended for Approval or Rejection
A review recommendation has been made. It is not the official decision.
Approved
Officially approved.
Rejected
Not approved. ASWRA will explain the reason by email.
