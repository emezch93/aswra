What we do

Strengthening the recycling sector

ASWRA works to organise, support and advance waste recyclers and recycling enterprises across Anambra State.

1

Sector organisation

Bringing eligible recyclers, recovery workers, aggregators, enterprises and cooperatives into a shared membership platform.

2

Capacity development

Promoting training, workshops, technical knowledge and safer, more responsible working practices for members.

3

Industry coordination

Encouraging cooperation across the recovery and recycling value chain and sharing relevant market and industry information.

4

Advocacy and sustainability

Facilitating constructive engagement on members’ concerns and promoting responsible resource recovery and environmental protection.

How ASWRA facilitates opportunities

Subject to its approved rules, available programmes, partner arrangements and eligibility criteria, ASWRA may facilitate or share information about opportunities such as:

Market and off taker linkages

Facilitating connections with recognised buyers and approved aggregation arrangements. Prices and terms are agreed transparently and may vary with the market.

Producer funded recycling programmes

Facilitating access to applicable extended producer responsibility (EPR) initiatives for eligible materials such as PET plastics and used beverage cans, where available and subject to programme rules.

Health insurance information

Information and help with applications to relevant health insurance programmes, including the Anambra State Health Insurance Agency (ASHIA), subject to that agency's eligibility and payment requirements.

Cooperative and thrift opportunities

Access to approved savings and credit initiatives through a duly established, authorised cooperative with its own constitution and financial rules.

Please note. Membership does not guarantee loans, grants, contracts, government appointments, producer payments or admission into any third party programme.

Environmental engagement

ASWRA supports environmental awareness and community engagement initiatives, including participation in relevant environmental observances and clean-up activities where organised.
