What we do

Our work

Waste management, advocacy and training for a clean and healthy Anambra State.

1

Waste collection

We collect waste from companies, homes and communities for proper disposal and recycling.

2

Advocacy and awareness

We tell people the disadvantages of not disposing of waste properly. ASWRA may also raise members' concerns about unlawful harassment, improper levies or unfair treatment with the relevant authorities.

3

Training and empowerment

We train and empower members on proper recycling and waste management, including responsible and safe working practices.

4

A clean and healthy Anambra

We work to keep Anambra State clean and healthy through responsible waste recovery and cooperation with communities and partners.

How ASWRA supports recyclers

Subject to the Association's rules, programme availability and eligibility, ASWRA may help recyclers with:

Market and off taker linkages

Facilitating connections with recognised buyers and approved aggregation arrangements. Prices and terms are agreed transparently and may vary with the market.

Producer funded recycling programmes

Facilitating access to applicable extended producer responsibility (EPR) initiatives for eligible materials such as PET plastics and used beverage cans, where available and subject to programme rules.

Health insurance information

Information and help with applications to relevant health insurance programmes, including the Anambra State Health Insurance Agency (ASHIA), subject to that agency's eligibility and payment requirements.

Cooperative and thrift opportunities

Access to approved savings and credit initiatives through a duly established, authorised cooperative with its own constitution and financial rules.

Please note. Membership does not guarantee loans, grants, contracts, government appointments, producer payments or admission into any third party programme.

Annual celebrations

Each year ASWRA marks World Clean Up Day, World Environment Day and other environmental days through clean ups and awareness activities.
