About ASWRA

Who we are

A membership association for waste recyclers and recycling enterprises in Anambra State.

The Anambra State Waste Recyclers Association (ASWRA) is a state-level membership association that organises, represents and supports waste recyclers and recycling enterprises across Anambra State.

ASWRA is a collective development platform for participants across the waste recovery and recycling value chain. Its role is to strengthen members, encourage responsible industry practices, facilitate collaboration and represent shared interests through constructive engagement with relevant stakeholders.

Institutional role: ASWRA is a membership association. It is not a government regulator, law-enforcement body or substitute for statutory waste management authorities.

ASWRA provides an organised platform through which waste sector operators can improve their professional standards, access relevant information, develop business relationships, take part in capacity building programmes and explore opportunities in the circular economy.

The Association recognises the contributions of informal waste collectors, community based recyclers, micro, small and medium sized recycling enterprises, cooperatives and other waste sector participants. It also works constructively with government agencies, local authorities, producers, recycling companies, development partners, financial institutions, civil society organisations and communities.

Vision

To become a leading, credible, and inclusive association advancing sustainable waste management, responsible recycling, environmental protection, and economic empowerment in Anambra State.

Mission

To organise, empower, and support waste recyclers and recycling enterprises through capacity development, digital innovation, cooperative initiatives, strategic partnerships, market access, and policy advocacy for a cleaner environment and a sustainable circular economy.

Core values

Unity

Encouraging cooperation, inclusion and peaceful coexistence among members.

Environmental responsibility

Supporting cleaner communities and sustainable resource recovery.

Economic empowerment

Creating opportunities for sustainable livelihoods and enterprise development.

Accountability

Ensuring responsible use of Association resources and accurate reporting.

Partnership

Building constructive relationships with public institutions, private enterprises, communities and development partners.

Sustainability

Advancing long term environmental, social and economic benefits.

Our objectives

  • Organise and coordinate waste recyclers and recycling enterprises operating in Anambra State.
  • Promote responsible collection, sorting, aggregation, transportation, processing and recycling of recoverable materials.
  • Encourage environmentally responsible waste management and the reduction of pollution.
  • Provide members with information on recycling markets, technologies, business opportunities and industry developments.
  • Promote training, workshops, seminars, technical assistance and professional development.
  • Facilitate access to legitimate recycling markets, aggregation centres, off takers and potential business partners.
  • Encourage waste sector cooperatives and other lawful mechanisms for savings, credit and enterprise development.
  • Promote access to appropriate financing, grants, insurance and other support, subject to eligibility and the requirements of participating institutions.
  • Develop and maintain an accurate membership database and encourage reliable reporting of recycling activities.
  • Advocate policies and programmes that improve the welfare, safety, productivity and business environment of waste recyclers.
  • Facilitate constructive engagement between members and government agencies, local authorities, producers, recycling companies and development partners.
  • Support sustainable collection and aggregation systems for recyclable materials.
  • Discourage the purchase of stolen materials, child labour, exploitation, environmental abuse and other unlawful practices.
  • Promote occupational safety, responsible working practices and access to health and social protection programmes.
  • Encourage the participation of women, young people, community groups and other eligible stakeholders in the recycling economy.
