Privacy notice

How ASWRA handles membership application information.

Draft for ASWRA review. This notice must be reviewed and approved by ASWRA, with legal advice where appropriate, before public launch. Items in [square brackets] must be completed by ASWRA.

Who is responsible for your information

The Anambra State Waste Recyclers Association (ASWRA), Staff Development Centre, Behind Old Government House, Awka, Anambra State, Nigeria. Contact: aswrahome@gmail.com or 07085379538.

What we collect

  • Your name or organisation name and, for organisations, a contact person.
  • Your email address, phone number, address and Local Government Area.
  • Your membership category and answers to the category questions on the form.
  • Your confirmations of the declaration and this notice, and the date you applied.

We do not ask for your NIN, identity documents, bank details or payments through this form.

Why we collect it

To process your membership application, to contact you about it, to let you check its progress with your reference number, and to keep records of the Association's decisions. [ASWRA to state its legal basis.]

Who can see it

Only the authorised ASWRA membership administrator can view applications. Your application reference lets you see your own status. It shows no personal details. We do not sell your information. The website is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes data on our behalf.

How long we keep it

[ASWRA to decide and state the retention period for approved and unsuccessful applications.]

Your rights and how to contact us

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or ask how it is used, by contacting ASWRA using the details above. [ASWRA to confirm the process and any complaint route, for example the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.]

Security

Applications are stored in a protected database. Access to the administrator area requires a password and is recorded. No system is perfectly secure, and we ask you not to include sensitive information in free text fields.
