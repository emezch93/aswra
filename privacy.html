Privacy notice

How ASWRA handles membership application information.

Who is responsible for your information

The Anambra State Waste Recyclers Association (ASWRA) is responsible for the personal information submitted through this website. The State Secretariat is at Staff Development Centre, Behind Old Government House, Awka, Anambra State, Nigeria. You can contact ASWRA at aswrahome@gmail.com or 07085379538.

Information we collect

When you apply for membership, we collect the information you enter in the online form, which may include:

  • Your name or organisation name and contact person, where applicable.
  • Your email address, telephone number, address and Local Government Area.
  • Your membership category and the answers relevant to that category, such as your organisation type, registration details or recycling activities.
  • Your acceptance of the applicant declaration and privacy notice, together with the application date and reference number.

The online application form does not request your National Identification Number, identity documents, bank details or payment card information. Do not include sensitive personal information in free-text answers or send it through an unverified channel.

Why we use your information

ASWRA uses the information to receive and assess membership applications, contact applicants about their applications, request further information where necessary, communicate review outcomes, operate the application-status service and maintain accurate membership and administrative records. The form records your confirmation that you have read this notice and agree to the stated use of your information.

Who may access your information

Access to application records is restricted to the authorised ASWRA membership administrator. Cloudflare provides the website hosting and database infrastructure and processes information on ASWRA's behalf to operate the service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect the rights, security or property of ASWRA, applicants or others.

Anyone who has your application reference number may be able to view the application status and any message ASWRA has made available through the status checker. The status checker does not display your contact details or the other personal information in your application. ASWRA does not sell personal information.

How long we keep information

ASWRA retains application and membership information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to review applications, administer membership, maintain appropriate Association records, meet applicable legal or administrative requirements and resolve disputes. When information is no longer required for these purposes, it will be securely deleted or de-identified, subject to any applicable record-retention obligation.

How we protect information

ASWRA uses access controls and technical safeguards designed to protect application records against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss. Administrator access requires authentication. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, so applicants should submit only information requested by the form and protect their application reference.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request deletion where appropriate, or ask questions about how your information is used. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent; this does not affect processing already carried out. To make a request, contact the ASWRA State Secretariat using the email address above. ASWRA may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You may also raise a concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where applicable.
