Make your enquiry useful

What to include when contacting ASWRA

A clear message helps the Secretariat understand your enquiry and direct it appropriately. You do not need to send sensitive documents or private identification details in an initial enquiry.

Membership enquiries

State whether you are applying as an individual, business, cooperative or institution. For an existing application, include your reference number so the Secretariat can understand which application you are asking about.

Partnership proposals

Introduce your organisation, explain the area of recycling or environmental work you are interested in and outline the kind of discussion you would like to have.

Training and programme enquiries

Describe the subject you are asking about and, where relevant, the type of participants or location involved. Programme dates and eligibility should be confirmed by the Secretariat.

Protect yourself when communicating

Use the official contact details published on this website. If you receive a request for payment, personal information or documents, verify it with the Secretariat through a known official contact before taking action.

Important safety reminder

Do not send your NIN, bank details, passwords or identity documents through the general enquiry form or an unverified WhatsApp contact. Do not pay an individual on the strength of an informal message. Any approved fees and payment instructions must be confirmed through official ASWRA channels.
